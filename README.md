# 🇵🇰 Pakistan Tech & AI Career Landscape 2026

An interactive research-based infographic exploring the **Software Development and AI career landscape in Pakistan**.

This project was created as part of my learning journey with **Google AI / Gemini Deep Research**, focusing on how AI is influencing software development, entry-level opportunities, technical skills, internship requirements, and modern career preparation.

## 📊 Project Overview

The infographic presents research insights across several areas:

* 🇵🇰 Pakistan's technology and AI landscape
* 🤖 Impact of AI and automation on software development
* 💻 In-demand programming languages and frameworks
* 🧠 AI, LLM, and modern development skills
* 🎓 Internship and entry-level requirements
* 💰 Salary and compensation benchmarks
* 🚀 Career preparation roadmap for students

## 🔎 Research Areas
